## EN — Marc Yohann's Mods, `community-ports`

Title: **Villager Life — villagers live whole lives (NeoForge 1.21.1 – 26.3)**

```
Mod Name: Villager Life
Mod Version: 1.0.0
Game Version: 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.8, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3
Mod Loader: NeoForge
Dependencies: none (JEI or EMI optional)

My port of Villager News, grown far past the original: much more to do, and all models, sounds, voices and lines regenerated.

Villagers work, build their houses and their town by hand from what they buy at the village stall with real coins, marry, raise children, grow old — and talk about all of it in English or Russian voices (one jar per voice language). A finished town raises the Spire of the Architects: a hundred floors with keepers and trials.

Install on the server and every client. Pick one jar: -en (English voices) or -ru (Russian voices).

Download (GitHub release): https://github.com/s00d/villager-life/releases/tag/1.0.0
Overview video: https://youtu.be/oe4SSerVNHs
Source and issues: https://github.com/s00d/villager-life
```
